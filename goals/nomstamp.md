# NomStamp (iOS)

**Slug:** `nomstamp` (matches `work/nomstamp/`)

**One sentence:**
Grow NomStamp — the food-stamp app where people capture what they eat and see it on a map for memory — into a real side product with **100 returning users**.

**Why this, now:**
I am already building this on iOS and spending real time on it. The point is a product people come back to, not “learn iOS” as a course. Learning shipping on iOS happens because the product needs it.

**What “done” looks like:**
100 users who use NomStamp more than once (come back and stamp again — not install-only, not a single tap). Capture → map memory is good enough that return use is natural.

**What I will not chase in the name of this goal:**
Endless polish or feature piles that do not help someone stamp food and return. Rewriting the stack or studying iOS for its own sake. Scale / monetization fantasies before 100 returning users. Letting NomStamp silently eat DevOps/English weeks without naming it on `now.md`.
